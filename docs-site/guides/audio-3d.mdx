## Audio generation

Open the generate menu in the bottom bar (the arrow next to **Image Generation**) and choose **Audio generation**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/audio-dialog.webp" alt="Audio generation dialog on the Voiceover tab" style={{ maxWidth: '480px' }} />
</Frame>

| Tab | What you give it | Model |
|---|---|---|
| **Music** | style tags (genre, mood, instruments, tempo), optional lyrics with `[verse]` / `[chorus]` lines, and a length of 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds | ACE-Step |
| **Voiceover** | the text to read and a voice: Chinese, American, British or Japanese, female or male. With an audio clip selected on the canvas, the voice list also offers **Clone the selected audio's voice** | Kokoro, IndexTTS-2 for cloning |
| **Sound effect** | a short description, up to 47 seconds | Stable Audio Open |

Each result is an audio card on the canvas with a play button, a seek bar and its length. Audio files you drop on the canvas become the same cards, and the Agent can make music, voiceovers and sound effects too.

</Frame>

## Image to 3D

Select an image of a single object, ideally on a plain background, choose **Image to 3D** under **⋯**, then **TRELLIS.2** or **Pixal3D**. ComfyUI cuts out the object, builds a textured mesh, cleans and simplifies it, and saves a GLB file. Pixal3D also estimates the photo's camera field of view with MoGe and generates with it.

If you have several views of the same object, select two to four of them, put them in a row and choose **Multi-view to 3D** on the selection toolbar. Left to right they are the front, left, back and right views; Pixal3D builds one model from all of them. The model is placed next to the image.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/image-to-3d.webp" alt="A mug photo and the 3D model made from it" />
</Frame>

The 3D card turns slowly by itself. Its toolbar can stop the rotation, set a background color, **Export Current View** as an image you can keep designing with, switch to **Interact** to orbit and zoom with the mouse, and download the GLB. 3D files you upload (GLB, GLTF, OBJ, FBX, STL, PLY) get the same card.

Generating a model takes a few minutes. See [Models](/guides/models#audio-and-3d) for the files.
